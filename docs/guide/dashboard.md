# Dashboard and structure

## Dashboard

Each chart on the dashboard answers a question, such as "Which surahs are the longest?", with a one-line answer computed from the range below it. There are twelve. The last one is the **mushaf map**: all 6,236 ayahs in order, one row per juz, each ayah as wide as its text and coloured by where it was revealed.

<img src="/shots/dashboard.webp" alt="The dashboard on a computer: the surah length chart with its answer, filters and export buttons" width="640">

- **Choosing a chart.** On a wide screen the questions are listed beside the chart. On a narrow one, **All charts** opens the list, and the `‹ 1 / 12 ›` stepper walks through them in order. You can also swipe sideways anywhere on the page.
- **Filters** are chips above the chart: the range (the whole Qur'an, or a span of surahs or of juz), which ayahs, the sort, the colouring and the bar direction. A chip you have changed is tinted and has its own clear button. **Reset** puts them all back.
- **Reading a chart.** With a mouse, click a bar or point to open the reader there. On a touch screen, the first tap shows a readout (title, detail, **Open in reader**), and a second tap opens it.
- **Show data** swaps the chart for its table. The first column opens the surah or ayah.
- **PNG** and **CSV** export the chart and its rows. In the Android app they go to the share sheet.

The link in the address bar keeps the chart and its filters, so you can share a view.

### The explorers

The **root explorer** shows every root in the corpus with its occurrences. The **topic explorer** browses a curated tree of topics. Both link to each ayah. The reader's context sheet links into them from any ayah's roots and topics.

## Structure

Structure shows how the mushaf is divided, in seven tabs:

- **Overview:** the totals, and the Makki and Madani split.
- **Juz**
- **Hizb**
- **Manzil**
- **Rukuʿ**
- **Similar ayahs:** pairs of ayahs that share their wording, as arcs.
- **Sajda:** the prostration markers.

Swipe sideways to move between the tabs. A tap on a division opens the reader there.
