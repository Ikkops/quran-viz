# Bookmarks

There are two kinds of bookmark, and each has a name and a colour you choose.

- **A pin** marks one place. Set it on another ayah and it moves there. It suits the places you come back to: where your tilawah stopped, your memorisation, the passage your study circle is on.
- **A collection** keeps any number of ayahs, each with an optional note. Use one for favourites, a theme, or the ayahs for a talk.

You start with one pin and one collection.

## In the reader

Each ayah row has a pin button and a bookmark button. Both open a sheet where you can:

- Put a pin on the ayah or take it off.
- Add the ayah to a collection or remove it, and write its note.
- Make a new pin or collection.

In the mushaf layout, the same buttons are in the ayah's popup.

## Finding them again

**Activity → Bookmarks** lists your pins and collections. You can rename and recolour them, delete them, and open any ayah. On Home, a last-read card shows the pins placed in its surah and how many of its ayahs are saved in collections. The dashboard's mushaf map can show a dot on every bookmarked ayah.

Bookmarks are part of the backup file and of sync (see [Settings](/guide/settings)).
