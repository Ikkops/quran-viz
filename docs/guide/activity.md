# Activity and khatam

## Reading time

The app counts reading time while the reader is open. Either the page is on screen and you have touched, typed or scrolled in the last two minutes, or recitation is playing (listening counts as reading, even with the screen off).

**Activity → Statistics** shows:

- **Today**, against your daily goal: 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 or 60 minutes.
- **Current streak:** days in a row with the goal met.
- **Longest streak** and **total time**.
- **Bars** per week, month, year or for all time.

Home shows today's time and the streak in one line, which opens Activity.

With [sync](/guide/settings#sync-between-devices) on, time read on your other devices is added in. Each device's time is kept apart, so none is counted twice.

## Khatam target

To finish the Qur'an by a date, set a khatam target in Activity → Statistics. Choose the start and end dates.

The app measures your progress by the mushaf page of your newest last-read place. Each day it shows four things:

- The pages left.
- The average per day you need.
- How many pages to read today to stay on track (0 once today's are read).
- A status: on track, behind by a number of pages, overdue, or done.

Changing the target starts the line again from today. On Home, a row under the reading line shows today's pages and opens the reader where you stopped.

## Reminders (Android)

Settings → Reminders has two daily notifications, each with its own time:

- **Daily reading goal:** skipped on a day you have already met it.
- **Memorisation review:** only on days with pages due, and it says how many.

The app asks for notification permission only when you switch one on.
