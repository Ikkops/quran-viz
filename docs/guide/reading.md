# Reading

## Opening the reader

Home lists the surahs, the juz or the manzils. Filter them by name or number. **Sort and filter** has three settings:

- **Sort:** mushaf order, revelation order (also as a timeline), ayah count or word count.
- **Place:** only Makki or only Madani surahs.
- **Layout:** a list or cards.

A tap opens the reader there.

Above the list, **Last read** shows where your recent stretches of reading stopped, newest first. Each surah appears once. Settings → Reading sets how many places to show.

You can also open the reader from almost anywhere: a chart, a search result, a bookmark, a memorisation plan, or a link someone sent you.

## The two layouts

The icon in the reader's top bar switches between two layouts.

**Ayah by ayah.** Each ayah is a row: its number (tap it for the ayah's context), then play, pin, bookmark and a menu to copy the text or a link. Translations, tafsir notes and transliteration sit under the Arabic.

<img src="/shots/reader.webp" alt="Surah Ya-Sin ayah by ayah on a phone, with the translation under each ayah and the player at the bottom" width="300">

**Mushaf pages.** Whole pages as printed. Choose the printing in the reader's settings: the Madinah mushaf of 1441 or 1405, or the 15- or 16-line IndoPak, or a flowing page that fits your screen. A tap on an ayah shows its translation, a play button and its context. On a printed page, press and hold a word to see that word's meaning, root and grammar.

## Moving around

- **Swipe** sideways to turn pages the way a mushaf turns: swipe right for the next surah, juz or page, and left for the previous one. The next pane is already in view while your finger moves.
- **Go to:** tap the title in the top bar to pick a surah, an ayah or a page.
- **Read by:** in the reader's settings, choose what one window holds: a surah, a juz, a hizb, a rukuʿ or a page. The reader reopens the ayah you were on in that division.
- **Search:** the magnifier finds words in the Arabic (it tolerates spelling differences) and in every translation on screen. Type `2:255` or a number to jump to an ayah. Step through the matches with the arrows, or Enter and Shift+Enter.
- On a keyboard, ← goes back and → goes forward.

The strip under the top bar shows where you are in the whole Qur'an.

## What you can show

All of this is in the reader's settings (the gear):

- **Translations:** one main translation, and up to a few more under it. Footnotes appear as small numbers; tap one to read it.
- **Tafsir:** a tafsir under each ayah, or several. The main one is also in the context sheet.
- **Transliteration**, in the spelling of your interface language.
- **Word by word:** each word's meaning in English or Indonesian, under the word.
- **Tajweed colours.** **Tajweed rules** opens a sheet that explains the stop signs and each rule's colour, with its trigger letters and an example word.
- The Qur'an font and its size. These are also in Settings.

## The ayah's context

Tap an ayah's number (or **Context** in its popup) to open a sheet with five tabs:

- **Tafsir.**
- **Word by word**, with grammar.
- **Asbab al-nuzul**, the occasions of revelation from the hadith collections, where they exist.
- **Similar ayahs**, with the words that differ tinted. You can hide them to test yourself.
- **Roots and topics**, linking to the dashboard's root and topic explorers.

**About this surah**, in the top bar, describes the surah at the top of the screen.
